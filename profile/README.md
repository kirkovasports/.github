## What we build

<table>
<tr>
<td width="33%" valign="top">

**One scoreboard**<br>
NFL, NBA, WNBA, NHL, MLB, soccer, UFC and NASCAR in one place: live scores, play-by-play and a win-probability chart for every game, on the web and on iPhone, with Live Activities and the Dynamic Island.

</td>
<td width="33%" valign="top">

**Picks that show their work**<br>
Game picks and player prop milestones (1+ shots on goal, 20+ points) sorted into **Sharp, Lean and Pass**, priced against real DraftKings lines, with a live bar to the line while the game is on. Every result is graded after the final whistle, misses included.

</td>
<td width="33%" valign="top">

**Fantasy, built in**<br>
Our own native fantasy: create or join a league, draft with friends, set lineups, trade and follow the matchup, for football, hockey, basketball and soccer. The Helper reads injuries and projections and files your waivers.

</td>
</tr>
</table>
